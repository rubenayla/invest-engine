# Disclaimer

This is research software. Nothing it produces is investment advice.

The company notes under `notes/companies/` are one person's written opinions,
recorded to make reasoning reviewable later. They carry dates. Many are months
old, the prices and valuations in them are stale, and the verdicts were never
revisited. Do not read them as current recommendations.

The valuation models are simplifications. Discounted cash flow, residual income
and the gradient-boosting models each embed assumptions that are wrong in
different ways, which is why the output shows them side by side and disagreeing
rather than reducing them to one number. Disagreement between models is
information; a single confident figure would not be.

You are responsible for your own decisions. Verify anything here against primary
sources before acting on it.
